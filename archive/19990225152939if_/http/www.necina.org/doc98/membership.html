MEMBERSHIP

As you might know, NECINA starts a new year at the annual meeting on 6/6.  With new board members and new officers in place, we take upon us to expand the services for our members in the new year. We still have the monthly SIG meeting, which this year will focus on new technologies like gigabit Ethernet, directory services, voice over IP, web-based management, etc.

Besides the SIG meetings, we are planning a technology summit in October, on the convergence of voice and data network, and two forums, Career Advancement forum in November and Entrepreneur and Venture forum in April.

That is the technology side of NECINA. On the social side, we will have a fall cruise outing in September and a New Year's party in February. We are also in the process of upgrading our web site for better membership services. The new web site will mainly be a member-only area where users log on for various information.

As usual, the SIG meetings, technology summit, and forums are free tocurrent members, and a deep discount will be offered to members for social events. Therefore, we encourage you to renew your membership. For the renewal, you will get the first-ever NECINA Annual Publication, which contains technical papers, event reviews and member directory. You can renew your membership at the next SIG meeting, or mail your check ($25) and membership form to: NECINA, P.O. Box 5522, Wayland, MA 01778. The membership form, for information update purposes, can be printed out from the NECINA web at http://www.necina.org/prntform.htm

If you have any concerns or suggestions in regards to the membership service, feel free to email Lei Cao at lcao@seussnt.stratus.com or Weixiang Chen at wchen@ascend.com. We are looking forward to seeing you at future NECINA events.
