Internal Organization

Executive Board Directors is the governing body of NECINA. The Board of Directors invites industry leaders and experts to serve as Advisors. There are 10 Officers, namely, President, Vice President, Executive Secretary, Chief Financial Officer, Legal Counsel, and Directors of Public Relations, Publications, Programs, Membership, and Technology. They are responsible for the planning and execution of NECINA's daily operations.

In additon, NECINA had formed seven Special Interest Groups and an Internet Task Force to serve your professional needs and technical interests.
