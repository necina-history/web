Special Interest Group (SIG) Seminars
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

NECINA Special Interest Group (SIG) will be divided into the following functional areas:  

·           Network Infrastructure

·            Telecommunication

·            Wireless Networks

·           Content & Media Technologies

·            Networking Software/Web technologies

·            Information Technology

·            Entrepreneurship

·           Market and technology opportunities in China & Asia

 

Network Infrastructure

The exponential growth of Internet and private IP network has changed the landscape of the both the public and private network infrastructure. Many new network applications are created, and many new network services will be created to support the needs of the applications. The changing demand from the applications and services will require new thinking on how the network is constructed, provisioned, and managed. The network infrastructure must be flexible, secure, and with quality of service (QoS) guarantees. Much of these problems remain to be solved.

 

Telecommunication

The rapid advance of optical fiber communication technologies has provided an abundance of bandwidth in the past several years. Recent attentions are shifted towards how to harness the raw bandwidth and bring benefits to the vast majority of consumers and businesses.  In particular, broadband access and intelligent optical networks are two important areas in telecommunications.

 

Wireless Networks

Wireless telephony has grown to rival its wireline counterpart; wireless LAN has made increased penetration into the enterprise. Wireless networks bring unique challenges and opportunities. The current wireless networks are designed for voice telephony applications. New technologies and standards are being created to provide integrated services to the mobile subscribers.

 

Content and Media Technologies

Information can be shared through the communication network. The digitalization of content has enabled the Internet as an effective media for content delivery. Online audio and video distribution has becoming more prevalent. New technologies and standards will dramatically improve the ways digital contents are the created, delivered and consumed.

 

Network Software/Web Technologies

The intelligence of any system lies in the software that controls its operation. The majority of Internet users view the network through the web. Application software drives the demand for network services. New tools, languages, and design methodologies can make writing software easier and better.

 

Information Technologies

In this age of information overloading, how to organize and process the vast amount information becomes critical. Would the client-server paradigm still be effective with increased computing power and bandwidth?  Information security and assurance will become increasing important.

 

Entrepreneurship

What is entrepreneurship? What is required to become a successful entrepreneur? Leadership, persuasion, perseverance are some of the key qualities. NECINA’s successful entrepreneurs can bring their own experiences to the table.

Market and technology opportunities in China & Asia

The rapid economic development in China and Asia in the past decades offers unique opportunities for NECINA members to explore market and technology opportunities across the Pacific. With deep rooted culture background from China, and newly acquired experience in the U.S. We can play important roles in bridging the technology and culture gaps between the two parts of the world.
