Conference Theme:

The conference will feature five distinguished speakers in the Storage networks technology areas.

Ilan Carmi, partner at Kodiak, will present "Investment opportunities in the data center market".

 Ilan will discuss the forces and dynamics of the data center market and how these issues are providing a fertile ground for new startups.  Much like the changes seen in the telecommunications infrastructure industry over the last decade, the data center is being streamlined to address the next bottleneck of business and IT infrastructure.  New technologies like Infiniband, iSCSI and architectures like SAN/NAS, Server virtualization are being developed and deployed to re-organize the data center.

Dennis Hoffman, Founder and President of Storigen, will present "Distributed Storage Networks: The Case for Network Storage at the Edge".

Storigen pioneered the industry's first Distributed Storage Network, a new class of infrastructure that dramatically improves the economics and performance of bandwidth-intensive applications by simplifying data management, storage and delivery of all types of data across distributed environments. Combining centralized management software with distributed edge devices that store, stream, file serve, cache and Web serve, Storigen's Distributed Storage Networking solution enables enterprise and service providers to deliver applications such as e-learning, online corporate communications, video-on-demand and workgroup collaboration without the high cost and complexity of point products or network upgrades. Founded in 2000, Storigen Systems is privately funded and based in Lowell, Mass.

 

Raju Bopardikar, Founder/CTO of Cereva Networks, will present "".

Douglas Cahill, Vice President of Product Management, BondERA, Inc., will present "Managing the Data Lifecycle".

Doug will discuss best practices for classifying and managing various data types throughout its lifecycle based on a Quality of Service (QoS) based approach to storage provisioning. The presentation will also include a look at how to plan the migration of the data to new storage network implementation.

Dave Therrien, co-founder/CTO of Centerra, will present "Storage Challenges Create New Product Opportunities".

Dave will cover trends in storage technology, storage challenges today, the incumbent and new vendors of storage, opportunities for the future.

About the Speakers:

Ilan Carmi, Partner of Kodiak Venture Partners.

At Kodiak, Ilan focuses on communications and semiconductor investment opportunities. He was the founding investor and active Chairman of the Board of Trebia Networks and is a member of the board and an active early investor in Egenera, BTI Photonics, and Tiaris.

Ilan’s career has included extensive entrepreneurial, engineering leadership, and general management in the chip, data, and communications markets. Prior to Kodiak, Ilan spent several years with Nortel Networks (as part of the Bay Networks acquisition), where he was Vice President and General Manager responsible for Bay Networks' router business, including multiple products, markets and acquisitions. Ilan also served at 3Com Corporation as Vice President and Business Unit Manager, responsible for 3Com's enterprise gigabit switching business. Before 3Com, he joined a start-up, Chipcom Corporation, where as Vice President of R&D he built a world class organization, and delivered two major platforms to the enterprise market. In addition, Ilan worked for Fibronics Ltd., in Haifa, Israel, where he was the Director for R&D and delivered the first Fiber Distributed Data Interface product to the market.

Ilan has a B.S.E.E. and an M.S.E.E. from the Technion (IIT - Israel Institute of Technology), and was a Captain in the Israeli Air Force for five years. He is a private pilot and member of the U.S. Civil Air Patrol.

Dennis Hoffman, Founder and President of Storigen.

Dennis Hoffman is a 10-year veteran of the storage networking industry with expertise in strategy, marketing and business development. Most recently, he led marketing and business development for the storage and networking business at Avid Technology, where he was responsible for business strategy, product management, product marketing and marketing communications. He was also co-founder of the Edgestreme Systems Division and pioneered Avid's foray into the streaming media infrastructure market.

Prior to Avid, Dennis was a manager at strategy consultant Marakon Associates, where he helped develop value-based strategies for Global 500 companies in the photographic, oil and gas, news distribution and data storage industries.

Dennis holds a BSEE from Union College and an MBA from Harvard University.

Mr. Bopardikar is Cereva's founder and CTO.   In this role he is responsible for the overall technical vision and strategy of the company.   Mr. Bopardikar brings a unique combination of technical knowledge, vision and entrepreneurial spirit to Cereva.  As the company founder and a member of the board he has been instrumental in helping to raise over $150M in funding.  Prior to forming Cereva 1998, Mr. Bopardikar has prior successful entrepreneurial experience as a founding member of Stone + Wire, which was acquired by Discreet Logic in early 1995.

Mr. Bopardikar has a diverse technical background.  As chief technologist for Discreet Logic's Product Development Group he was responsible for product strategy and development of both storage and networking infrastructure.  Previously, Mr. Bopardikar had development experience with high performance networking and real-time systems in both distributed and parallel architectures at companies such as Thinking Machines, IBM, Eaton, and AT&T Bell Labs. 

This broad background enabled Mr. Bopardikar to see the market need for high performance, scalable storage systems, and provided the technical building blocks that are embodied in Cereva's products.  Cereva's storage systems are a combination of high speed networking, distributed and parallel processing, file system and storage technology derived from the combined knowledge Mr. Bopardikar and of the team he helped to build.

Douglas D. Cahill, Vice President of Product Management, BondERA, Inc.

Doug has 15 years of industry experience working with networking, database, and storage related products and technologies. As Vice President of Product Management at BondERA, Doug is responsible for developing the product strategy and roadmap for the company’s forthcoming database networking appliance. Prior to BondERA, Doug lead the definition of Sun Microsystems's StorageONE storage management software product family. Doug joined Sun vis-a-vie the $400m acquisition of HighGround Systems where, as Vice President of Product Management, he lead the product strategy for HighGround's industry-leading Storage Resource Manager (SRM) family of products and services. Doug also held senior product management positions at Shiva Corporation and Progress Software. Doug is a graduate of the University of Massachusetts, Amherst, MA.

Dave Therrien - CTO - Centerra Systems

For the past 23 years of his engineering career, Dave has been involved in the data storage industry.  Prior to founding Centerra Systems in 2002, he was VP of Software Development at StorageNetworks Inc.  Before that he held various engineering management,  and development positions at HighGround Systems, EMC, Epoch Systems and Data General. Dave holds a BSEE degree from Northeastern University.
