NECINA Back to China Delegation

Last year, NECINA and CINA organized a joint delegation and participated "The 4th Convention of Overseas Chinese Scholars (OCS) in Science & Technology" held in GuangZhou, China. The feedback we got from our NECINA participants and all parities were very positive and many members had expressed great interests in such an activity and hoped NECINA/CINA to organize similar activities again.

We are happy to announce that China Beijing International Hi-Tech Exposition will be held from May 22 ~ May 25 in Beijing, China. NECINA and CINA are organizing another delegation to participate this event. In addition to this event, NECINA and CINA will also participate "The 2002 Chinese Enterprise Science and Technology Innovation Conference (Hangzhou)", and Hi-Tech Exchange and Exposition Conference/Fairs in Shanghai and Ningbo from June 2 - June10. Here are specific time tables:

  • China Beijing International Hi-Tech Exposition:

Date: May 22 ~ May 25 in Beijing, China.

Location: Beijing Capital Grand Hotel

Registration Information: http://www.necina.org  (also check on http://www.hightechbj.com)

  • The 2002 Chinese Enterprise Science and Technology Innovation Conference

          Date: June 2 - June 4, 2002

          Location: Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China

          Registration information: http://www.necina.org

  • Meeting with Shanghai Pudong Hi-Tech Development Zone Government/Companies and Visits

          Date: June 5 - June 7, 2002

          Location: Shanghai Pudong, China

          Information: http://www.necina.org

  • Zhejiang Investments and Trade Conference (Fourth Session) and Overseas Chinese Ningbo Start-a-Business Tour 

          Date: June 8 - June 10, 2002

          Location: Ningbo, Zhejiang, China

          Information: http://www.necina.org

This delegation will be comprised of NECINA/CINA members who have an interests in exchanging and cooperating with Chinese Science and Technology, Networking and related industries, research institutes, universities, companies, especially if they have particular projects that they would like to bring back to seek opportunities in Mainland China, or Greater China Region.

Qualification and Registration Information:

NECINA members in natural science and high-tech industry: (engineering, computer science, networking etc.), business, education, finance and law Entrepreneurs from US companies as well as venture capitalists, are qualified to registered for these events.

Registration Deadline and Trip Selection:

Deadline: April 19th, 2002. PLEASE NOTE THAT: You must state which event your are attending (up to all four events) and download forms for these event at our web site http://www.necina.org

Some potential benefits to our members:

  • A chance to find proper business partners in domestic China
  • Possible free meals and accommodations for attendees for local costs
  • Possible traveling allowances (3000 Yuan) might be available (through reimbursement). It will depend on whether you have projects to bring back. Please fill up the forms contained in above web sites (an central web site is http://www.necina.org) . If you are interested in these events, please submit your applications and resume (bio) in English and/or in Chinese version with related project information to JChen@unispherenetworks.com,  charliewang@attbi.com, kzheng@cereva.com, Sylvia_Zhang@kopin.com. Please note you must adhere to the application form guidelines regarding the projects.

The NECINA organizing team will go over all applications and select about 10 members,  then mail it to the convention organizing committee immediately for evaluation. Once your application is approved, the convention organizing committee will issue an official invitation letter to you for your visa application.

If you have any questions please email Charlie Wang, Kevin Zheng and Sylvia Zhang at JChen@unispherenetworks.com, charliewang@attbi.com, kzheng@cereva.com, Sylvia_Zhang@kopin.com.
