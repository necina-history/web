Making a Career Move as an Independent Consultant
 

Abstract:
Kenda Systems is a $50 million, worldwide Information Technology consultancy. With offices in the US and Europe, Kenda specializes in providing clients with top quality software engineering and IT professionals. Kenda has been in business since 1984, and currently has over 350 consultants working worldwide.

The purpose for this session is an introduction to consulting. The specific topics will include:
Introduction to Boston area job market
Industry and technology trends
Introduction to consulting
Pros and cons of consulting
Ideal consultant profile
Questions and answers
