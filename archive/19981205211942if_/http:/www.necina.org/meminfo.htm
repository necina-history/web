Membership Information

Membership Benefits

  • Receive E-Mail announcements and monthly newsletters.
  • Attend meetings and seminars.

  • Expand your network of contacts through NECINA's global alliances.

    2) Mail us your $25 membership fee and your Chinese name. Donations are greatly welcomed and appreciated.
