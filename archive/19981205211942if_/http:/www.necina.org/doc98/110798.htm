Third NECINA Technical Conferenceon

"Multi-Service Networks: Convergence of Voice and Data Networks"

Chelmsford Radisson Hotel Conference Center

Chelmsford, MA

November 7, 1998

 

NECINA is pleased to host a special half a day industry wide technical conference at Chelmsford Radisson Hotel on Saturday, November 7, 1998. The conference will be open to all NECINA members free of charge and for non-NECINA members at $25 per seat. A reception will be provided at the end of the conference for speakers, NECINA officials and attendees.

Conference Theme:

The conference will feature 3 distinguished speakers in the software and networking industries on the general subject of "Multi-Service Networks: Convergence of Voice and Data Networks". The subject will be examined from several perspectives. Paul Saverino, former Chairman of Bay Networks, will be the lead speaker in the conference to share his vision on the convergence of voice and data networks from application services to infrastructure. Michael Hluchyj, Founder of Sonus Networks, will give a technology update and challenges as IP and telephone networks continue to converge. David Schantz, now a partner at Matrix Partners and formerly Director of Product Marketing at Cadia Networks, will review the subject from a market perspective from emerging CLEC services to migration of existing infrastructure.Steve Gardell, Manager of the Enhanced Value Services group at GTE Internetworking will share his insight from a backbone/service provider perspective.
These discussions are to propose high-level technology roadmaps for the future and to inspire others in the making of next generation web enabled technologies as a whole.

About the Speakers

As one of the most prominent entrepreneurs and executives on the East Coast, Paul Saverino has had a string of phenomenal successes that have had far reaching impacts to the data networking industry in the past decade. After leaving Prime Computer, Paul launched InterLAN to pioneer Ethernet based communications front-end systems to time-sharing hosts in the early '80 and moved on to found Wellfleet Communications in the late '80 on the eve of router evolution to the Internet. Paul engineered one of the first megamergers by merging Wellfleet and SynOptics into Bay Networks which was recently acquired by Nortel.

As a technology visionary, Mike Hluchyj spent years at Motorola/Codex where he managed an advanced development group within its Network Product Division. Mike joined Summa Four to become their CTO after Codex. After Summa Four, which was recently acquired by Cisco Systems, Mike founded Sonus Networks in Westford. The company specializes in the development of carrier class voice over IP networking devices.

David Schantz is a general partner at Matrix Partners in Waltham, Massachusetts and serves on the board of Convergent Networks. Prior to joining Matrix, David was the founding Director of Product Marketing for Cadia Networks which was acquired by Fore Systems. Prior that Cadia, David works at Bay Networks in Billerica, Massachusetts.

Steve Gardell joined GTE Laboratories in 1996 where he is a technical manager leading a group of system architects and software developers responsible for the development of "enhanced value services" for GTE customers.

Cheng Wu is founder and CEO of ArrowPoint Communications. Before starting ArrowPoint, Cheng was co-founder and VP of Engineering for Arris Networks which was acquired by Cascade Communications. Cheng will share his view on how the Internet can become web aware.
