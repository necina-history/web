First NECINA Career Forum
at
Chelmsford Radisson Hotel Conference Center, Chelmsford, MA
December 12, 1998

NECINA is pleased to host a special half a day Career Forum at Chelmsford 
Radisson Hotel on Saturday, December 12, 1998. The forum will be open to 
all NECINA members free of charge and for non-NECINA members at $25 per 
seat. A reception will be provided at the end of the forum for speakers, 
NECINA officials and attendees.
Forum Theme:
The forum will feature four distinguished speakers, all of them have very 
outstanding career in their own fields.  Their talks will focus on their 
experience and the career choices.
From his perspective as an entrepreneur, Mr. Woo will discuss the career 
paths for entrepreneurs in engineering. Opportunities, individual choices, 
philosophy, individual values, and some case studies based on real 
experiences will be presented. Mr. Woo will investigate with the audience 
the process for creating vision, the process of invention, and an example 
of observing technology for purpose of stimulating thoughts.
Ms. Lau will be sharing some of her experience of starting a business. 
 Some of the decisions and challenges along the way such as financial, 
growth strategies and employees retention. She will also touch on lessons 
she learned and to get some thoughts and feedback from the audiences 
regarding leadership and management in a high-tech environment.
Ms. Roselle's presentation will focus on those who are interested in 
becoming more satisfied with their career whether you are in a large or 
small company. Technical competence is always a necessity to succeed. 
However, Ms. Roselle will speak mainly about the aspects of success that go 
beyond technical competence.
Mr. Chuang will discuss the changing nature of work, specifically the rise 
of independent contractors in the work force. Challenges and opportunities 
of working as a free agent will be presented.  Lastly, Mr. Chuang will 
describe how he is dramatically redefining his company to help people 
succeed as independent professionals.

About The Speakers:
Mr. Leon Woo is president, CEO and co-founder of Tenor Networks, a startup 
company founded in August, 1998. From January 1994 to August 1998, he was 
VP Engineering of Switching Division and S2 Division at 3Com Corp. Before 
joining 3Com Corp, Mr. Woo was VP and co-founder of Synernetics, Inc. which 
was a pioneer and market leader in the LAN switching industry. Synernetics 
was acquired by 3Com in 1994. He also held various posts at Apollo Computer 
Inc., Aetna Telecommunication, Raytheon, Northeastern University and MIT.
Mr. Leon Woo holds S.B. and S.M. degrees in Electrical Engineering and 
Computer Science from MIT.
Ms. Joanna Lau is a founder and the president of Lau Technologies, a full 
service company providing engineering, manufacturing, software development 
and system integration services to military and commercial customers.
Ms Lau holds a BS degree from SUNY at Stony Brook, an MS degree from Old 
Dominion University and an MBA degree from Boston University.
Ms Barbara Roselle is a Director of Engineering in the Optical Networking 
Group at Lucent Technologies.  She is responsible for ATM/IP development on 
Bandwidth Manager, data communications protocols and access multiplexor 
 applications within this business unit. Ms. Roselle has had a wide variety 
of device, hardware and software development positions in AT&T and Lucent 
Technologies Bell Laboratories that span customers worldwide.
Ms. Roselle holds a BS in Electrical Engineering from Cornell University 
and an MS in Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan.
Mr. John Chuang is a founder and the president of MacTemps, Inc. The 
company started as a temporary help firm but is in the midst of changing 
itself to a company that helps independent workers build careers and 
financial independence. MacTemps' revenues exceeds $130 million with 
operations expanding as far as Tokyo.
Mr. Chuang graduated from Harvard University. In addition, he hods an MBA 
degree from Harvard Business School.
