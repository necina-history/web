Harvard Chinese Students & Scholars Association (HCSSA) Presents:

E-COMMERCE AND YOUR SUCCESS

SPEAKER: Mr. Edward Soong, Anew Technology Corporation in Burlington, Mass. USA

Time: November 21, 1998 (Saturday);  2:30 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Location: Harvard Yenching Library Auditorium (see below for directions)
Looking back on human history, one sees that we have evolved from an 
agricultural society into an industrial society, and from an industrial 
society into an enterprise society.  Today, we are evolving yet again into 
an information age at an astonishing speed.  The advent of Internet 
heralded this latest revolution, and has made a great and far-reaching 
impact on everyone's life.
In this seminar, the speaker will examine the major aspects of this great 
revolution.  Taking advantage of the explosion in Internet and e-commerce 
creates new opportunities for everyone.  On the other hand, for those who 
don't know how to ride on this new wave will certainly be washed away by
it.  So, What is e-Commerce?  Where is the big wave?  What does it mean to 
business, to science, to society, and to humanity?  Why should you care? 
What can you do?  How to participate and benefit from it?  What to do to be 
successful?  If you want to survive and get ahead in the new
millennium, if you want to know the new rules of the game in life, if you 
want to explore the unlimited opportunities in e-commerce, no matter where 
you are, this seminar is for you.  Please join us in this talk to find some 
thoughts and answers.
Content:    	Introduction
                A New Paradigm
                e-Commerce Defined
                e-Commerce Applications
                e-Commerce Components
                Success Stories
                Trend and How to Participate
Speaker Biography:
Ed is the founder and President of Anew Technology Corporation in 
Burlington, Mass.  The company's focus is in Internet/e-Commerce System 
Integration and Application Development.  It also runs a Cyber Marketplace 
- "AsiaMart?" at http://www.AsiaMart.net with one thousand businesses
from 64 countries.  Previously, Ed worked for Wang Laboratories, Inc. as 
the Senior Director of Business Development in Asia Operations.  Ed has 
over 29 years of experience in engineering, management, sales, and 
marketing in the Electronics and Information Technology industries.  Ed 
holds a B.S. in Electronic Engineering, a M.E. in Electrical Engineering, a 
M.S. in Computer Science, and a M.B.A.

PUBLIC.
