Dear NECINA member:
Please join us for our 2nd Annual Meeting on June 6, 1998 at the ACAS 
facility in Woburn, Massachusetts.
We have invited two distinguished speakers for this important event. Wufu 
Chen, our chairman will give a speech on emerging technologies in the voice 
over data networks and Dev Gupta, a Cisco Vice President, will give a 
speech on the local loop technologies that include xDSL, cable modem and 
other high speed data access technologies.
We also plan to elect some new board members and recognize those who have 
made significant contributions to NECINA in the past year. Lastly, we 
would like to introduce you NECINA President-elect Edward Soong and his new 
team.
At our annual meeting, you can also renew your NECINA membership and 
receive an updated copy of our first-ever NECINA member directory, thanks 
to all the work by many NECINA staff members.
A reception party will be held at the end of our program.
I look forward to seeing you all.
Best Regards,
Cheng Wu
NECINA President, 1997-1998
