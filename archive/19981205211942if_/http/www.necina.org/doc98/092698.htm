Towards a Global Virtual Data Center

- A working model and on-going project at Harvard-MIT Data Center

Abstract:

The Harvard-MIT Data Center (HMDC) is the main social science data repository of the Harvard University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. For the past two years, it has been providing data services through an on-line Virtual Data Center (VDC) which automates a complete line of services incliding data searching, ordering, receiving, cataloging, conversion, sub-setting, on-line analysis, and user support. This presentation will introduce the background information about techniques and operation of VDC. An on-going project aimed at populating VDCs to form a global network of virtual data centers and commercial data providers will also be discussed. General concept and approach about knowledge management will also be covered. The objective of the talk is to present to the udience one of the real world applications using some of the popular technology, such as web interfaced database, on-line data analysis, and knowledge management. It is intended to trigger discussion in the audience about the subject and related issues.

Presenter:

Dr. Yi (William) Wei is the Executive Director of the Harvard-MIT Data Center. Prior to HMDC, he worked as a Senior Research Data Analyst and System Manager at the Data Coordinating Center, School of Public Health, Boston University. He has a Ph.D degree in Oceanography from the University of Delaware, and he worked at MIT as a post-doc and research associate before converted into an IT professional.
