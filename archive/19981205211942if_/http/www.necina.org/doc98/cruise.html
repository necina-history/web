Boston Harbor Cruise

    Take a boat cruise around the water front! It's ideal for getting to know members of NECINA, and family outing. We are charting a small boat suitable for up to 90 passengers, one enclosed, heated deck with an open bow area.

Date:         Saturday, September 12, 1998
Time:         2 pm to 4 pm
Fee:          $6.00 member, $10.00 non-member. Children under 5 is free.
              Not recommended for small toddlers.
Registration: Limited to 50 people on first come-first serve basis.

Parking Information:

Parking is available across the Northern Ave bridge. Meters are free after 6:00 pm and all day Sunday. The parking lots on Northern Ave are $6.00 all day. No overnight parking.

TO GET TO THE PARKING ON NORTHERN AVE:

FROM POINTS NORTH - Take Route 93 South to the High Street / Congress Street exit. Make a left onto High Street at the first light. Turn left onto Congress Street (second left). Go over the expressway (Rt. 93) and make a left onto Atlantic Avenue. Next light is Northern Ave, parking on left over bridge.

FROM POINTS SOUTH - Take Route 93 North to the Atlantic Ave / Northern Ave exit (first exit out of the South Station Tunnel). Stay to right (sign reads "Northern Ave / Rowes Wharf") and go straight through light onto Northern Ave. Go over bridge and parking is on the right.

PARKING IS ALSO AVAILABLE UNDER THE BOSTON HARBOR HOTEL. WHILE IT IS VERY CONVENIENT - IT IS ALSO VERY EXPENSIVE. MASSACHUSETTS BAY LINES DOES NOT VALIDATE PARKING.

We will be departing from Rowe's Wharf. Here's the direction:

Directions to Rowes Wharf

From Points North

Take Route 93 South to the High Street exit, then left on High St., then take the left at the second traffic lights on Congress Street, across bridge over I-93 and left on Atlantic Avenue, and it's two blocks to Rowe's Wharf. There is parking in the Boston Harbor Hotel garage or on Northern Avenue. Walk through the arch at the Boston Harbor Hotel, and look to your right. Mass Bay Lines is located at Gate "C."

From Points South

Take Routes 3 and 93 North to the Atlantic Avenue exit. Stay to the right for parking on Northern Avenue or to the left for the Boston Harbor Hotel garage. Continue North on Atlantic Ave. for one block to Rowe's Wharf. Walk through the arch at the Boston Harbor Hotel, and look to your right. Mass Bay Lines is located at Gate "C."

Via the T

Take the Red Line to South Station. Walk North on Atlantic avenue for two blocks to Rowe's Wharf.

OR

Take the Blue Line to Aquarium. Walk South on Atlantic Avenue for two blocks to Rowe's Wharf.

Tour buses and trolleys tours stop at Rowe's Wharf, and it's an easy walk from Quincy Market, the Financial District, and Downtown Crossing.
