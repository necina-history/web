Web Operations Management

Ensuring the Performance and Availability of Commerce Critical Web Sites

Abstract:

The Internet has paved the way for the deployment of web-based mission-critical systems, with web servers and application components linking to systems across multiple platforms and networks. Companies have web sites that are critical revenue sources, but lack tools to keep the web applications accessible and performing well. Current management solutions fail to address the specific needs of web-centric applications such as traffic management, process control and content replication. This evolution of business on the Web has changed thedemands on corporate Web administrators and strategists. Not long ago, they were praised for the successful deployment and promotion of the company's Web site. Today, they are often held accountable for an ever-increasing line of revenue, all while juggling infrastructure requirements, operational complexity and 24-x-7 availability. Through ongoing research with 10 of the country's top commerce sites, we found that six critical needs exist for effective Web operations management. This session will discuss these critical needs,and the requirements for guaranteeing a high level of service for your critical web applications
