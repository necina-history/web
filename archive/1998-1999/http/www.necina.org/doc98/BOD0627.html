NECINA

Minutes of the Meeting of

Board of Directors

________________________________________________________________________

A meeting of the Board of Directors of New England Chinese Information and Network Association, Inc. was held on the 27th of June, 1998 , at 10 AM in Westford, Massachusetts as set forth in notice of the meeting.

The following directors were personally present:

Hsiu-Li Chen, Chi Shue, Edward Soong, Cheng Wu, John Zao

being a majority of the members of the Board of Directors of said corporation.

 

  1. The meeting was call to order and it was moved, seconded and carried that Mr. Cheng Wu act as Chairman of the meeting, and Mr. Ed Soong act as Secretary of the meeting.
  2. Three director-elects were present and thereupon consented to served as Board Directors for a three-year term ending June 30, 2001.
  3. Upon motion duly made, seconded and carried by two-third majority, it was RESOLVED that an amendment to bylaws establishing the office of Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors is adopted.
  4. Upon motion duly made, seconded and carried, the Board of Directors thereupon proceeded to elect Mr. Wu-Fu Chen as its Chairman for fiscal year 1998-1999.
  5. Upon motion duly made, seconded and carried, the Board of Directors proceeded to elect Mr. Cheng Wu as its Vice Chairman for fiscal year 1998-1999.
  6. Upon motion duly made, seconded and carried, the Board of Director proceeded to elect Mr. Ed Soong as the President for fiscal year 1998-1999 and to approve Dick Chiu as Secretary and Angela Ho as Treasurer for said fiscal year. Both are appointed by Mr. Ed Soong.
  7. NECINA’s Plan for 1998-1999 was presented by Mr. Ed Soong and approved by the Board. Policy and operation related issues were discussed and directives were given for the management team to carry out. One particular item was the financial report of NECINA. It should be reported to the Board periodically so that the Board can take any supportive actions necessary to ensure the financial health of NECINA.

 

There being no further business to come before the meeting, on motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.
