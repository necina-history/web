An introduction to
IP Telephony with current issues and applications

Abstract:
IP telephony has attracted a tremendous amount of attention over the last couple of years. IP telephony is rapidly evolving from both a technology and a business perspective. I introduce the technology with a review of key system components, protocols, and operational concerns such as QoS. IP telephony is being applied in a number of different business and product contexts. I review different applications of IP telephony with special attention to what GTE is planning to deploy. Finally the presentation will review important current trends. Where is the technology going?

Presenter:
Steven Gardell joined GTE Laboratories in 1996 where he is a technical manager leading a group system architects and software devlopers. The development activitity aims to provide "enhanced value services" to GTE Customers. These are services that that are of higher value than the core IP transport on which they are layered. Mr. Gardell has 18 years of professional software development experience, most of it the CAD/CAM industry. He has a Master's Degree in software engineering and a BS in Civil Engineering. He is a member of the IEEE and the ACM.
