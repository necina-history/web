High-Speed Internet Router - Architecture & Applications

Abstract:

Internet traffic is more than doubling each year driven by new applications, such as business VPNs, Web and video distributions. New transport technology (WDM and DWDM) and emerging broadband access techniques(ADSL, Cable modem) also provide significant pressure on building new Internet infrastructure.In this presentation, we focus on an important networking device -high-speed IP router for building Internet core infrastructure. We present router architecture and it's technology trend. We discuss the key technology components for building high-speed routers, including, IP forwarding engine, high-speed interconnect/fabric, and elements for supporting Differentiated Service (DiffServ) or Quality of Service (QoS).

Presenter:

Nanying Yin joined Bay Networks, Inc. in 1993, where he is Director and General Manager of Internet Core Routers, where he leads the development of Internet core router products. Prior to his current role, he was Director of Networking Technology in Bay Architecture Labs where he was responsible for the technology development in switching, IP packet forwarding architectures and traffic management mechanisms for the next generation internetworking products.
