Chelmsford Radisson Hotel Conference Center
Chelmsford, MA

NECINA is pleased to host a special half a day industry wide technical conference at Chelmsford 
Radisson Hotel on Saturday, March 28, 1998.  The conference will be open to all NECINA members 
free of charge and for non-NECINA members at $15 per seat.  A reception will be provided at the 
end of the conference for speakers, NECINA officials and attendees. RSVP through 
necina@necina.org recommended due to limited seating. 

Conference Theme:

The conference will feature 4 distinguished speakers in the software and networking industries on 
the general subject of “Web Computing: The Next Paradigm Shift”.   Each speaker will share his or 
her vision and insight on how the shift toward web computing will shape how new applications and 
networking technologies are deployed to enable full scale E-Commerce over the Internet.  These 
presentations will assess the impacts of emerging technologies such as next generation Web based 
development environment, Smart Internet, and the convergence of server, web applications and 
networking technologies.  These discussions are to propose high-level technology roadmaps for the 
future and to inspire others in the making of next generation web enabled technologies as a whole.

About the Speakers

Known as the visionary of Content Smart Internet or Active Networks in the Internet market 
research community, Paul Callahan first coined the term of Content-Smart Internet when he headed 
up the Networking group at Forrester Research in Cambridge. In his new role as VP of Business 
Development at Bay Networks, Paul brings the intimate knowledge of emerging technologies and 
companies poised to have an impact to the Internet in the next decade.

Formerly founder and CEO of Watermark Software, David Skok has grown start-ups into $100M+ 
companies and developed market leading technologies. David will share with us how web based 
groupware technologies will shape the future of applications development.

As one of the original architects of Lotus Notes and the founder of Bright Tigher, an emerging 
leader in the area of web computing system tools, Kathy is uniquely qualified to share with us her 
vision on the convergence of server, application and networking technologies in the new web 
computing era.

Cheng Wu is founder and CEO of ArrowPoint Communications. Before starting ArrowPoint, 
Cheng was co-founder and VP of Engineering for Arris Networks which was acquired by Cascade 
Communications.  Cheng will share his view on how the Internet can become web aware.
