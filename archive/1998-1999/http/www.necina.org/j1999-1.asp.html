President's Report

Dear Members:

It's been another exciting year for NECINA. We started our new Entrepreneur Forum and Career Forum with good acceptance from our members. These new conferences will provide very valuable information to advance our member's career and their businesses. We have also co-operated with other Chinese professional organizations to hold seminars jointly to promote the wellbeing of Chinese professionals in the New England area. We have got out of Massachusetts and gone to New Hampshire and Rhode Island.

We are so glad that our membership system is finally online. Today, new members can register online and old members can update online. An updated membership directory is conveniently available online at any time. In the past year, we have also tried a Boston harbor cruise to provide an additional gathering opportunity for our members to socialize among us. The result was definitely worth our efforts.

Fiscally, we have a strong year too. Our income for the past year as of June 8, 1999 was $17,122.84. With the expenses of $14,550.30, we have $2,572.54 surplus. We want to thank WuFu Chen's membership matching fund and all the people and organizations who donated to NECINA in the past year. The total donation for last year was $8,850.00.

As you know, NECINA is of the members, by the members, and for the members. Without the countless volunteered efforts and help from our members, there is no NECINA. We thank you, all of the volunteers out there. Personally, I would like to express the great honor and pleasure for me to work with you all in the past year.

Last year this time, I asked you what NECINA could do for you. Now is the time, I would like to ask you what you can do for NECINA. I urge you to give your continued contribution and support to NECINA. I thank you.

Edward Soong
