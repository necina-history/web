"CHINESE YUAN-SHIU FESTIVAL PARTY"


NECINA would like to invite you, your family and your friends to join us to
celebrate the Chinese Yuan-Shiu Festival on Saturday, February 14th, 1998
at the AMERICAN CHINESE ART SOCIETY (ACAS) in Woburn, MA. (Addr.: 29
Montvale Ave. Woburn, MA and Tel.: 617-938-9900)

The evening will start with a buffet dinner followed by performances,
karaoke and dances. It will be a great opportunity for us to come to
know one another away from our work.

Please respond to the NECINA email address necina@necina.org and mail your
check to NECINA mailbox (NECINA, P.O.Box 862, Westford, MA 01886) to have
your reservation before Jan. 31st, 1998 with the number of adults and children
attending. Party tickets will also be available from all NECINA officers. You
may purchase the tickets from anyone of them.
