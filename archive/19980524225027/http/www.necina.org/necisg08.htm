Business & Financial Planning
Abstract:
As the Internet grows daily in its popularity and size,
small businesses and startup companies mushroom to explore
new technologies and business opportunities. This seminar
aims at providing an overview of  business & financial
planning to help entrepreneurs to understand the scope of
developing his/her own business from a financial perspective
and the resources available to them.

Presenter:    Dr. Jared Keyes
Dr. Keyes is currently a member of the Board of Directors of
NECINA and works for a private bank in Boston. Previously he
worked for the Bank of Boston as a VP. Dr. Keyes received his
Ph.D. from Harvard University.
