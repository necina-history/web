Title: 
        Open Forum for Advanced Networking Topics
 
             (with Professor H. T. Kung) 

Abstract:
        This seminar is given in an open forum format where topics of 
        interest are sent to Prof. Kung from members of NECINA prior to 
        the seminar. Prof. Kung will then prepare his seminar based on 
        the questions and topics he receives. Topics submitted should 
        be generally defined in the areas of advanced networking, ATM, 
        and Internet. Please send your questions and interest directly 
        to Prof. Kung at 
                kung@deas.harvard.edu
        or call at 
                (617)496-6211

Presenter:
        Dr. H.T. Kung
        Prof. Kung is a professor at Harvard University and a member of 
        the Board of Directors of NECINA. Prof. Kung is a well known 
        scholar and researcher in a broad spectrum of academic fields 
        such as telecommunication and networking.
