Membership Information

NECINA accepts Individual Membership at $10/year. Corporate Membership is also available. If you have questions or need more information about NECINA, please contact Sam Shen (Director of Public Relations and membership), (508)-436-8261, necina@anewnet.com.

Membership Benefits

  • Receive E-Mail announcements and monthly newsletters.
  • Attend meetings and seminars.
  • Receive discounts on admission to NECINA's conferences and technical workshops.
  • Expand your network of contacts through NECINA's global alliances.

    How To Become a Member of NECINA

    1) Submit the On-Line Membership Application Form, or if your web browser isn't forms-capable, print out this form, fill it out, then fax or mail it to us.
    2) Mail us your $10 membership fee and your Chinese name. Donations are greatly welcomed and appreciated.
