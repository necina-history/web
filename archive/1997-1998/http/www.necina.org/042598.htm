Multimedia Internet = Voice + Video + Networking + ... 

Abstract:

Dr. Shih-Wei Shiue will talked to us about the audio, video and 
networking technologies that make Multimedia Internet a reality. He will
also 
share with us his experience of nurturing InterVision throughout its first 
year.

        InterVision was form march 97 by Andrew Chiang and Wei Shiue. The
company is working in the area of Audio conferencing, Video conferencing. It
now has 30 people and one audio conferencing product. The video product
will be available late this year.

        Some of the key technology includes audio compression, audio echo
cancellation, video procession, video compression and video post process.
Some of the technology has already been used by internet telephone company
"Clarent". The technology includes dynamic bandwidth allocation, package
loss recovery and of coures audio comparession and echo cancellations.
Clarent has bit Lucent in winning AT&T contract as the "Internet Phone 
Gateway".

        InterVision is now in its second phase of funding and is looking
for many talent engineers. Come and talk to them. Dr. Shuie will also show 
their H.263 video demo and audio conferening phone at the talk.
