Internet Routing: A historical perspective
 

Abstract:

 The Internet is a rapidly changing environment, with new protocols and 
 services appearing virtually overnight. To understand where the 
 Internet is today, it is useful to see how the Internet has evolved. 
 This talk will focus on the evolution of Internet routing, including 
 addressing, routing architecture, routing protocols, and the services 
 (such as multicast and security) that the Internet's routing system 
 provides.

Presenter:

 Mr. Moy is a Senior Consulting Engineer at Ascend Communications Inc. 
 Mr Moy is the author of the OSPF and MOSPF protocol specifications, 
 and currently chairs the OSPF and MOSPF Working Groups in the Internet 
 Engineering Task Force. Mr. Moy has been involved in the design and 
 development of router software for 15 years, currently at Ascend, and 
 previously at Bolt, Beranek and Newman and Proteon, Inc.
     
 Mr. Moy holds a Master of Arts in Mathematics from Princeton 
 University (1977), and a Bachelors of Engineering in Mathematics from 
 the University of Minnesota (1976).
