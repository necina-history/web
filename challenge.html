NECINA 30-Year Web Challenge

Welcome to the NECINA 30-Year Web History Discovery Challenge! This project aims to reconstruct three decades of organizational legacy using modern AI tools and agentic workflows.

The final reconstructed website will be published and hosted at: necina-history.github.io/web/

📜 Background & Context

Founded in 1996—just one year after the birth of Netscape and Internet Explorer—NECINA (New England Chinese Information and Networking Association) grew alongside the internet and web.

At the time, there was no cloud and there were internet downturns. The early web landscape lacked cloud computing and suffered from fragile, self-hosted servers with single-point failures. Because the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine did not capture everything, significant portions of NECINA's digital footprints are missing or fragmented.

All is not lost! As we approach the NECINA 30-Year Anniversary, we want to uncover this lost history, honor our traditions, and preserve our spirit for future advancement.

🎯 The Challenge

The NECINA AI Club is leveraging Agentic tasks by tools such as OpenClaw to solve this puzzle. Your mission is to:

  • Discover: Find the simplest, most efficient ways to curate past NECINA events and organizational changes for each fiscal year.
  • Reconstruct: Rebuild a historical narrative and structure that best represents the original form of the website.
  • Fill the Gaps: Piece together fragmented text, broken links, and missing timelines that the Internet Archive left behind.

🛠️ How to Contribute

We welcome students, developers, historians, and AI enthusiasts of all skill levels to collaborate!

  1. Explore the Data: Check out our initial dataset compiled from archive.org in this repository.
  2. Fork and Branch: Create a fork of this repository or establish your own development branches.
  3. Build Agents: Deploy OpenClaw or other AI tools to automate data scraping, synthesis, and layout reconstruction.
  4. Submit a PR: Propose your data additions, script improvements, or structural changes via a Pull Request.

Getting started? Check our Initial Data Repository to dive right in.

🎁 The Reward

This is an open-source, community-driven initiative. The true reward is the hands-on learning process. By participating, you will:

  • Master cutting-edge AI agentic workflows and automation tools.
  • Gain deep, practical experience in web archeology and data curation.
  • Preserve a foundational piece of the New England Chinese tech community's history.

Let's build the future by reconstructing our past!
